Upon execution of an authorized Statement of Work (SOW) and settlement of designated milestone disbursements, full 100% Foreground Intellectual Property and Git production repositories transfer unimpeded to Client.

01

Acceptance of Terms

By engaging YESS Bangla Private Limited ("YESS Bangladesh"), accessing yessbangla.com, provisioning client cloud portals, or countersigning any Statement of Work ("SOW"), the counterparty entity ("Client") confirms binding acceptance of these Institutional Terms. If acting on behalf of a corporation, you warrant statutory signing authority.

Scope: yessbangla.com • Client Portal • API Endpoints • Enterprise SOWs
02

Services & Statements of Work (SOW)

All engineering services, cloud architectures, and venture deliverables shall be formally governed under discrete, countersigned Statements of Work. Each SOW delineates engineering sprints, milestone schedules, acceptance velocity, and resource allocations.

Formal Change Order Procedure:

Modifications to scope, architectures, or delivery timelines require an authorized bilateral Addendum or Change Order prior to task execution.

03

Fees, Invoicing & Statutory Taxes

Invoicing schedules follow milestone approvals as outlined in individual SOWs. Standard remuneration terms require net settlement within fourteen (14) calendar days of formal invoice submission. Dual denomination is supported in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) and US Dollars (USD).

account_balance National Board of Revenue (NBR) Compliance:

Invoices are subject to applicable statutory deductions including Advance Income Tax (AIT) and Value Added Tax (VAT) per Bangladesh Finance Acts. Withholding challans must be remitted to YESS within thirty (30) days of statutory deduction.

04

Acceptance & Milestone Verification (UAT)

Upon completion of an engineering milestone, Client receives seven (7) business days to evaluate deliverables against documented User Acceptance Testing (UAT) specifications. In the absence of documented defects within this evaluation window, acceptance is deemed unconditionally granted.

Severity-1 and Severity-2 defects reported within the UAT period are remediated by YESS Bangladesh at zero auxiliary charge.

05

Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Key Guarantee
lock_open Complete Foreground Ownership Guarantee

Upon final settlement of milestone disbursements, YESS transfers 100% of all Foreground Intellectual Property—including bespoke source code, database architectures, algorithmic logic, and UI assets—unconditionally to Client.

Background IP & Frameworks: Pre-existing reusable utility libraries retain YESS licensing, granting Client a perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable commercial license for continuous operations.

Repository Handover: Delivery entails full push to Client-designated bare-metal or cloud Git repositories with complete commit history, automated CI/CD manifests, and configuration documentation.

06

Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

Both parties agree to treat all algorithmic designs, financial records, client identities, database schemas, and commercial strategies as Strictly Confidential. Neither party shall disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent.

07

Warranties & Performance Standards

YESS warrants that software engineering shall adhere strictly to IEEE industry baselines and ISO 27001 secure development lifecycles. All bespoke deliverables include a 90-Day Post-Launch Defect Warranty, commencing immediately upon final UAT sign-off.

08

Limitation of Liability

Except for gross negligence or willful statutory infringement, neither party shall be liable for indirect, punitive, or consequential damages. Aggregate institutional liability under any claim arising out of an SOW is expressly capped at the total amount disbursed to YESS Bangladesh under that specific SOW during the preceding 12 months.

09

Governing Law & Dhaka Court Jurisdiction

These Terms and any individual SOW shall be governed by and construed under the statutory substantive laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

Dispute Escalation Protocol:

1. Mandatory 30-day senior counsel amicable negotiation.

2. Binding arbitration under the Bangladesh Arbitration Act 2001, seated in Dhaka.

3. Exclusive judicial forum: Competent Courts of Dhaka.

10

Force Majeure & Subsea Cable Disruptions

Neither party shall be in default for delivery delays resulting from acts of God, civil emergencies, sovereign telecommunication blackouts, or documented SEA-ME-WE undersea fiber disruptions impacting Bangladesh international internet transit gateways.
