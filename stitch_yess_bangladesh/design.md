# YESS Bangladesh Design System

## 1. Brand Essence & Aesthetics
YESS Bangladesh (yessbgd) is an institutional venture builder and multi-industry conglomerate in Bangladesh.
The aesthetic is high-trust, cinematic, and modern:
- Deep oceanic teal base paired with liquid champagne gold accents.
- Translucent liquid-glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`) with ultra-fine border highlights.
- 3D embossed minted coin medallions for flagship ventures (Yess Soft, Shondhaan, Yess Organic Haat, Akash OTT) with metallic reeded edges and water reflection pools.
- Water ripple ambient background with deep gradient overlays.

## 2. Color Palette & Semantics
- **Brand Teal (Primary):** `#0d6e6e` - Sovereign corporate authority and technological excellence.
- **Teal Glow (Primary Accent):** `#35b0aa` - Active state indicators, highlights, and borders.
- **Champagne Gold (Secondary):** `#d4a359` - Premium prestige, enterprise milestones, and award citations.
- **Gold Glow (Secondary Accent):** `#f6c87a` - Floating tags, badges, and hover focus rings.
- **Ocean Navy (Dark Backdrop):** `#061a1b` - Hero section overlays, top navigation utility bar, and footer base.
- **Canvas / Surface:** `#f8faf9` - Crisp off-white canvas with pure white frosted glass cards.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, Extra Bold (800) and Bold (700) with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`).
- **Body & Controls:** `Inter`, Regular (400), Medium (500), and Semi-bold (600).
- **Bangla Support:** Native Unicode line-height relaxed to 1.6 with normal tracking.

## 4. Key Components
- **Top Utility Banner:** Dark navy bar with hotline `+880 1805-464343`, email `yessbangla.bd@gmail.com`, and `EN | BN` pill toggle.
- **Glass Header:** Translucent sticky navbar with corporate crest, mega navigation, search, and teal 'Let\'s Talk' action button.
- **Flagship 3D Coins:** Circular embossed medallions with milled reeded perimeter and reflective puddle underneath.
- **Bilingual Download Cards:** White glass action cards for downloading company profile in English & Bengali (PDF / DOCX).
- **Mobile Navigation:** Docked floating 5-item bottom bar with active teal glow pill.
