Institutional Venture & Client Framework

This Master Terms of Service ("Agreement") is executed by and between YESS Bangla Private Limited, an institutional corporate venture builder incorporated under the Companies Act 1994 with Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC) under registration C-184920 (hereinafter referred to as "YESS Bangladesh", "we", "us", or "our"), and the contracting entity or institutional enterprise client (hereinafter "Client", "Counterparty", or "you").

Key Legal Summary:

Upon execution of an authorized Statement of Work (SOW) and settlement of designated milestone disbursements, full 100% Foreground Intellectual Property and Git production repositories transfer unimpeded to Client.
01

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing, browsing, or provisioning services across yessbangla.com, authenticating through the YESS Bangladesh Enterprise Client Portal, utilizing our secure API endpoints, or signing a mutually accepted Statement of Work ("SOW"), you expressly agree to be legally bound by this Agreement in full.

If you represent a banking institution, corporate conglomerate, public sector entity, or international donor, you warrant with statutory authority that the signatory holds complete corporate charter power to bind said legal entity to these contractual obligations.

02

Services & Statements of Work (SOW)

Each bespoke engagement—ranging from cloud architecture, enterprise platform engineering, AI integration, to institutional venture co-building—shall be formalized in a discrete, countersigned Statement of Work (SOW).

Every SOW specifies the precise technical scope, architectural deliverable schedules, resource allocation tiers, sprint velocity expectations, and payment schedules. In the event of direct conflict between this general Terms of Service and an individual signed SOW, the terms within the designated SOW shall prevail for that specific scope.

change_circle
Formal Change Order Procedure: Any deviation, feature expansion, or pivot beyond the accepted sprint boundaries will prompt a formal Change Request (CR) document detailing cost impact in BDT/USD and revised delivery timelines prior to engineering initiation.
03

Fees, Invoicing & Statutory Taxes

Invoices are issued in dual denominations: Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) for domestic counterparties, and United States Dollars (USD) for international clientele. All payments are strictly payable within fourteen (14) calendar days from the electronic transmission of invoice.

Value Added Tax (VAT)

Invoices exclude VAT unless stated. Domestic deliverables adhere to statutory Value Added Tax laws under the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act 2012 overseen by the National Board of Revenue (NBR).

Advance Income Tax (AIT)

Where client-side withholding of AIT applies under the Income Tax Act 2023, valid withholding tax treasury challans must be furnished to YESS Bangladesh within thirty (30) days of deduction.

Unsettled invoices past thirty (30) days incur statutory financing interest at 1.5% per month or the highest rate permitted under Bangladeshi statutory law, without prejudice to our right to suspend ongoing software development iterations.

04

Acceptance & Milestone Criteria

Upon sprint or phase completion, YESS Bangladesh shall deploy the code deliverable to a designated staging sandbox. Client is granted a seven (7) business-day defect evaluation period to conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT) against the agreed functional requirements.

If no reproducible, critical Severity-1 or Severity-2 defects are submitted in writing via the tracking dashboard within the 7-day period, the milestone deliverable is deemed automatically accepted, initiating subsequent milestone billing.

05

Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

terminal Complete Foreground Ownership Guarantee

Upon final settlement of all applicable invoices under the specific Statement of Work, YESS Bangladesh unequivocally transfers 100% of all Foreground Intellectual Property, proprietary business logic, bespoke source code, and assets to the Client.

Background Tools & Libraries: YESS Bangladesh retains ownership of its pre-existing core venture frameworks, scaffolding modules, build scripts, and standard developer toolkits ("Background IP"). We grant the Client an irrevocable, perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive license to utilize, compile, and maintain such Background IP embedded directly within the delivered product.

Git Repository Handover: Production GitHub/GitLab repositories, container orchestration manifests, automated CI/CD pipelines, and environment secrets are formally transferred directly to client-owned cloud infrastructures with zero proprietary vendor lock-in.

06

Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

Both parties agree that all confidential information, including technical roadmaps, algorithmic specifications, unreleased product features, customer PII, and financial terms, shall remain rigorously guarded under strict bilateral non-disclosure protocols.

Neither party shall disclose Proprietary Information to third parties without prior written consent, except to accredited legal counsel, auditors, and engineering personnel strictly on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality terms. This clause survives termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

07

Warranties & Performance Standards

YESS Bangladesh warrants that all services will be executed in a highly professional, workmanlike manner compliant with institutional international software engineering standards (IEEE/ISO 27001 baseline).

90-Day Post-Launch Defect Warranty:

We provide a comprehensive ninety (90) day warranty post-production deployment, during which any reproducible software bugs or non-conformities to the original SOW will be remediated at zero additional cost to the client.

DISCLAIMER: EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS WARRANTIES SET FORTH HEREIN, ALL DELIVERABLES AND CODE SAMPLES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS". WE DISCLAIM ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR UNSTATED PURPOSE.

08

Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable laws of Bangladesh, neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, special, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, enterprise data disruption, or goodwill.

Aggregate Liability Cap:

Each party's maximum total aggregate liability arising under or related to this Agreement or any Statement of Work shall be capped at the total amount actually paid by the Client to YESS Bangladesh under the specific SOW in dispute during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the incident.

09

Mutual Indemnities

By YESS Bangladesh: We agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Client against any third-party civil claims alleging that our bespoke authored foreground software deliverables directly infringe upon registered patents, copyrights, or trade secrets within the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

By Client: Client indemnifies YESS Bangladesh against claims stemming from client-supplied digital assets, third-party proprietary data models, unlicensed APIs mandated by Client, or unauthorized use violating the Acceptable Use Policy.

10

Term & Termination

This Agreement remains in active effect until terminated by either party. Either party may terminate an active SOW for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice, provided the breaching party fails to cure such breach within the thirty-day cure period.

In the event of termination, the Client shall pay for all milestone deliverables completed and pro-rata sprint hours logged up to the official termination date. Orderly asset transition, code export, and cloud secret migration shall occur within fifteen (15) business days.

11

Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for delivery delays or failure of performance resulting from events beyond reasonable institutional control. Such events explicitly encompass international submarine cable severance (SEA-ME-WE 4 / SEA-ME-WE 5 outages), catastrophic national grid collapse, civil unrest, statutory emergency orders, epidemics, and severe cyclonic storms.

12

Acceptable Use Policy & Sovereign Security

Counterparties engaging with YESS Bangladesh infrastructure agree not to perform uncoordinated penetration attacks, DDoS stress tests, or reverse-engineer pre-release binaries without executed testing agreements.

All platforms deployed under this framework strictly conform to the Cyber Security Act 2023 of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, ensuring sovereignty and zero unauthorized data egress beyond national territorial directives for sensitive banking/fintech data.

13

Governing Law & Dhaka Court Jurisdiction

This Agreement, its construction, validity, and performance shall be governed solely and exclusively by the substantive laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

Amicable Escalation & Arbitration:

In the event of any contractual controversy, senior executives shall first engage in good-faith amicable mediation for a mandatory duration of thirty (30) days. Failing amicable resolution, the dispute shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Dhaka under the Arbitration Act 2001 of Bangladesh. The competent Courts of Dhaka shall hold exclusive territorial jurisdiction.

14

Changes to Terms

YESS Bangladesh reserves the statutory prerogative to amend these master terms to reflect evolving legal frameworks or cloud operational practices. For enterprise clients holding signed active SOWs, formal 30-day prior written notice will be provided via electronic transmission to registered administrative contacts.
