Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Do shop-floor workers need to be tech-literate? expand_more

No. Terminals are engineered specifically for low-literacy use with intuitive Bangla voice prompts, visual iconography, high-contrast color-coded status, and one-touch barcode/RFID scanners. Factory operators typically master daily logging in under 15 minutes.

Can the factory terminals work completely offline? expand_more

Yes. Every shop-floor terminal buffers all telemetry locally in embedded SQLite databases and syncs automatically with zero data loss when LAN or Wi-Fi connectivity is restored.
