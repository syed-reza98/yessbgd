Do you work with organizations outside Dhaka?

64 Districts Maritime Ports Export Hubs
expand_more

Yes, actively. While our Executive Command and R&D wings are located at Motijheel and Gulshan-2 in Dhaka, we run nationwide deployment pods across all 64 administrative districts of Bangladesh. 05

What pricing structures and billing terms do you offer?

Milestone Turnkey Dedicated Agile Pods BDT / USD Clearing
expand_more

We structure pricing through three institutional models depending on project predictability and long-term roadmap requirements:

  • Fixed-Price Milestone Contracts: Ideal for well-scoped platforms. Capital is invoiced strictly against pre-agreed User Acceptance Testing (UAT) milestones.
  • Dedicated Agile Pods (Retainer Squads): Cross-functional pods (Product Lead, Backend Architect, Frontend Specialist, QA, DevOps) working exclusively on your product roadmap on a predictable monthly retainer.
  • SLA & SRE Evolution Retainers: Tier-3 production support covering high availability, security hardening, and minor functional improvements.

Banking Channels: Invoices are settled natively in BDT via Bangladesh Bank EFT/RTGS protocols or via USD wire clearing for foreign parent corporations.

08

How do you handle client data sovereignty, privacy, and infrastructure security?

Tier-4 BNDC ISO 27001 Zero-Trust
expand_more

Sovereignty is the cornerstone of YESS Bangladesh. We architect exclusively under Zero-Trust and Sovereign Residency Principles:

  • Domestic Residency: Production data resides within sovereign Tier-3 and Tier-4 domestic data centers (Kaliakoir National Data Center, Mohakhali, or private institutional on-premise clusters) ensuring zero foreign extraterritorial jurisdiction.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Strict alignment with Bangladesh Bank ICT Security Guidelines, the National Data Protection Ordinance, and ISO/IEC 27001 protocols.
  • Cryptography: AES-256 GCM encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit with client-held Hardware Security Module (HSM) keys.
