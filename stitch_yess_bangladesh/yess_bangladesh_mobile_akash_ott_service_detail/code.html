Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a production OTT launch take? expand_more

A standard broadcast-ready deployment takes 12 to 20 weeks from architecture discovery to store approval. This includes end-to-end player customization, DRM security keys, and MFS payment reconciliation.

Can you integrate local Bangladeshi payments? expand_more

Yes. We provide native integrations for bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay along with domestic & international card schemes (Visa, MasterCard, AMEX) and automated recurring tokenization.

Do you offer 24/7 post-launch SRE support? expand_more

All turnkey builds include a 90-day comprehensive warranty and 24/5 SLA. For major broadcasters and high-traffic cricket/sports events, we offer dedicated 24/7 mission-critical SRE retainers with 15-minute response times.
