Clarity & Implementation

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

psychology

Do shop-floor workers need to be tech-literate?

No. Terminals are engineered specifically for low-literacy use with intuitive Bangla voice prompts, visual iconography, high-contrast color-coded status, and one-touch barcode/RFID scanners. Factory operators typically master daily logging in under 15 minutes of guided onboarding.

wifi_off

Can the factory terminals work completely offline?

Yes. Every shop-floor terminal buffers all telemetry locally in embedded SQLite databases and syncs automatically with zero data loss when LAN or Wi-Fi connectivity is restored. External internet downtime never stops sewing or packing operations.
