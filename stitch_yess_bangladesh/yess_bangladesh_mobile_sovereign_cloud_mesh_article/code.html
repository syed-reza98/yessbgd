The Sovereign Imperative in National Digital Services

Over 84% of institutional digital traffic inside Bangladesh historically routed through upstream regional transit nodes in Singapore, Mumbai, or Frankfurt before returning to local subscribers. This dependence creates systemic vulnerability: undersea cable outages (SEA-ME-WE 4 & 5) routinely throttle mission-critical banking transactions, citizen services, and telecom interconnects.

Furthermore, domestic enterprise cloud expenditures continue to drive millions of dollars in unhedged foreign exchange outflows annually. Building an interconnected, autonomous domestic compute substrate is no longer speculative—it is the foundational pillar of national economic resiliency.
